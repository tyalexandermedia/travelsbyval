Overview

Why Asia belongs on your list

Asia is where trips become stories. Cherry blossoms drifting over Kyoto lanes. Long-tail boats in Thailand's limestone bays. Bali villas with private pools for less than a standard Caribbean room. Street food in Hanoi that ruins takeout forever, and Singapore's skyline glittering above it all.

It's also the region where planning matters most: seasons flip by coast, internal flights stitch countries together, and the gap between a tourist-trap hotel and a transcendent one is invisible on booking sites. That's where I come in: I sequence the route, time the seasons, and match every stay to how you actually travel.

Timing

Best times to visit

November – February

Prime time for Thailand, Vietnam & Cambodia: dry, cooler, and glorious: book 8–12 months out.

March – May

Japan’s cherry-blossom spring: iconic, popular, and worth planning a year ahead.

June – September

Bali’s dry season and Japan’s summer festivals; monsoon elsewhere, routing matters.

October – November

Japan’s fiery autumn foliage and shoulder-season value across the region.

Insider Tips

What I tell my clients

  • ✦Fly into one city and home from another. Open-jaw tickets save a full travel day in Japan and Southeast Asia, and I price them automatically.
  • ✦The seasons flip by coastline: Phuket and Koh Samui have opposite monsoons, so the right island depends on your dates, not the other way around.
  • ✦Japan runs on reservations: the best restaurants, museums, and even some temples book out weeks ahead. I lock the must-dos before you land.
  • ✦Luxury goes further here, a private driver in Vietnam or a villa with staff in Bali often costs less than a mid-tier U.S. hotel.

FAQ

Asia questions, answered

When is the best time to visit Southeast Asia?
November through February brings dry, cooler weather to Thailand, Vietnam, and Cambodia: the sweet spot for most first trips. Bali runs on its own rhythm, with April through October the driest months. I time every itinerary around the regional seasons so you never land in monsoon week.
How long should a first trip to Japan be?
Ten to twelve days is the sweet spot: Tokyo, Kyoto, and one side of the country's character: Hakone hot springs, Osaka food streets, or Hiroshima. Shorter is possible, but you'll spend a bigger share of it in transit. I build the rail passes, timed reservations, and neighborhood picks into one clean plan.
Do I need visas for Asia?
It varies by country and changes often: Japan, Thailand, and Singapore are visa-free for U.S. travelers on typical stays, while Vietnam and others have entry requirements. I verify current documentation for every traveler on your booking before you fly.
Is Asia good for a honeymoon?
Exceptional: Bali private-pool villas, Thai island resorts, and Japanese ryokans deliver five-star romance at prices that often undercut the Caribbean. Say 'honeymoon' when we plan: upgrades and amenities follow.
