Overview

Why cruising belongs on your list

Here's the truth about cruising: the ship *is* the destination, and lines differ as much as hotel brands. The megaship with a go-kart track and the intimate ship with a butler on every deck both sail the same Caribbean, and attract entirely different vacations.

I match you to the line, ship, itinerary, and even the specific cabin location that fits how you travel, then layer on advisor perks like onboard credit and upgrades. First cruise or fiftieth, the difference is night and day.

Insider Tips

What I tell my clients

  • ✦Cabin location is a science: midship-low for motion sensitivity, aft for wake views, never under the pool deck. I pick precisely.
  • ✦The advertised fare is rarely the real comparison: drink packages, Wi-Fi, gratuities, and excursions change the math by line. I compare total cost.
  • ✦Book the next cruise while onboard pricing is tempting, but route it through your advisor to stack perks.
  • ✦River and ocean cruising are different vacations entirely; plenty of travelers who 'don't like cruises' love rivers.

FAQ

Cruises questions, answered

How do I choose the right cruise line?
By matching the line's personality to yours: megaship energy versus small-ship intimacy, family programming versus adults-focused luxury, included-everything versus à-la-carte. The free consultation identifies your fit fast. It's the single highest-value decision in cruising.
Is a balcony cabin worth the extra cost?
In scenic regions like Alaska, the Mediterranean, and rivers: usually yes. On port-intensive Caribbean runs where you're rarely in the room, an oceanview can be the smarter spend. I advise per itinerary.
What does a cruise travel agent cost?
Nothing extra. Advisors are compensated by the cruise lines, and you frequently pay less overall once I apply promotions, group rates, and onboard credit that booking sites don't stack.
Ocean cruise or river cruise: what's the difference?
Ocean cruises are floating resorts with sea days and big entertainment; river cruises are intimate journeys through a region's heart, docking in town centers daily with excursions included. Different rhythms, both wonderful. I help you pick.
