Good Questions

Everything you’re wondering

Does it cost anything to use a travel advisor?
Nothing. Hotels, airlines, cruise lines, and resorts pay InteleTravel advisors a commission that is already built into the price you would pay booking directly, so you get a dedicated, certified expert at no extra cost, and often extra perks like onboard credit and room upgrades on top.
Why book with an advisor instead of a booking site?
A booking site shows you everything; an advisor shows you what’s right. I save you hours of research, match you with trips that genuinely fit your style, secure advisor-only perks, and become your single point of contact if flights cancel or plans change. You travel with a professional in your corner.
What kinds of trips does Val plan?
Ocean and river cruises, all-inclusive Caribbean and Mexico resorts, European vacations, Asia, Hawaii and Alaska, Disney and Universal theme-park trips, honeymoons and romance travel, family vacations, and group travel, destination weddings, milestone birthdays, and multi-generational reunions.
How far ahead should I start planning?
For the best selection and pricing: 6–12 months for cruises and all-inclusives, 8–14 months for Europe and honeymoons, and 6+ months for Disney. Last-minute? Reach out anyway. Advisor relationships often surface options the booking sites don’t show.
Where is Travels by Val located: and do I have to be nearby?
I’m based in St. Petersburg, Florida and work with travelers across Tampa Bay and nationwide. We plan together by phone, WhatsApp, and email, so wherever you live, I’m one message away.
What happens after I submit an inquiry?
I personally review your trip details and reach out to schedule your free consultation, a relaxed conversation about your travel style, dates, and budget. From there, I get to work designing your options. No pressure, no obligation, no spam.
